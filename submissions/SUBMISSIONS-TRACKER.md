## Strategy

Two versions of the piece will be needed:
- **Full-length (~2,800–3,000 words)** — for magazines/journals in Tiers 2–3 below, most of which expect this length.
- **Condensed (~964 words)** — for regional op-ed pages in Tier 1, which enforce hard word limits. Drafted: `Life-and-Death-condensed.md`. Note: SCNG-style outlets wanting 500–700 words will need a further trim.

Almost every outlet below requires **exclusive, unpublished** submissions — do not send to more than one at a time within the same tier/category. Narrative Magazine is the one confirmed exception (explicitly allows simultaneous submissions).

Because the anniversary hook has a hard date, sequence by lead time:
1. **This week:** Tier 1 (regional/CA op-ed pages) — fast turnaround, can still land near Sept 17.
2. **In parallel, now:** Tier 2 & 3 (national magazines/journals) — these run 3–6+ months behind on response time and mostly don't need the exact anniversary date to justify the piece; treat them as evergreen personal-essay submissions rather than anniversary news pegs.
3. Tier 4 — longer shots, submit opportunistically once higher tiers have responded (most are also exclusive).
