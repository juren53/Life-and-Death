Newspaper in the LA area that accept op-ed submissions

Several major regional newspapers, niche publications, and hyper-local community outlets accept op-ed (guest commentary) 
ubmissions in the Greater Los Angeles area:

Major Regional Publications
Los Angeles Times

Focus: Broad local, state, national, and international issues.  
LA Times

Word Count: 750 – 1,000 words.  
LA Times

Submission Method: Email full drafts (no pitches) to oped@latimes.com. Put the author's name and topic in the subject line, 
paste the text in the body (no attachments), and include a short bio with phone numbers.  

LA Times

Policy: Must be strictly exclusive to the LA Times.  
LA Times

Southern California News Group (SCNG)
(Includes the Los Angeles Daily News, Pasadena Star-News, Long Beach Press-Telegram, Torrance Daily Breeze, San Gabriel Valley Tribune, and Whittier Daily News)

Focus: Local Southern California policy, community issues, regional infrastructure, and state affairs.

Word Count: ~500 – 700 words.

Submission Method: Email drafts directly to the opinion editors via the online portal or city-specific opinion desk emails (e.g., opinion@dailynews.com).

Policy: Exclusivity required within the regional market.

Statewide & Public Policy Publications (LA Coverage)
CalMatters

Focus: California policy, politics, economy, and quality-of-life issues with state or regional impact.

Word Count: Max 650 words.  
CalMatters

Submission Method: Via their web submission portal or commentary@calmatters.org.

Policy: Must be exclusive. Published pieces are often syndicated across member papers throughout LA and California.  
CalMatters

Capitol Weekly

Focus: Public policy, regulation, and political governance affecting California.  
Capitol Weekly

Word Count: Max 750 words.  
Capitol Weekly

Submission Method: Submitted strictly via the online proposal form on capitolweekly.net.  
Capitol Weekly

Industry & Niche Outlets
Los Angeles Business Journal

Focus: Business, local commerce, real estate, technology, and economic policy impacting LA County.

Word Count: ~750 words.  
Los Angeles Business Journal

Submission Method: Email to letters@labusinessjournal.com or directly to the Managing Editor.  
Los Angeles Business Journal

Daily Bruin (UCLA)

Focus: Topics relevant to higher education, local community issues, and campus/regional developments.

Word Count: 400 – 900 words.  
Daily Bruin

Submission Method: Email to opinion@dailybruin.com.  
Daily Bruin

Hyper-Local & Community Outlets
Signal Tribune (Long Beach / Signal Hill)

Focus: Hyper-local issues, community developments, and regional perspectives localized to Long Beach/South Bay.  
Signal Tribune

Word Count: Max 1,000 words.  
Signal Tribune

Submission Method: Email completed drafts to newspaper@signaltribune.com.

L.A. Downtown News

Focus: Urban development, culture, housing, transit, and local policy in Downtown LA.

Word Count: ~500 – 800 words.

Submission Method: Email the editor at editor@downtownnews.com.

Key Best Practices Before Submitting
Always Submit Complete Drafts: Most papers (especially the LA Times and Signal Tribune) reject query pitches and only evaluate complete manuscripts.  
LA Times

Observe Exclusivity: Do not send an op-ed to multiple outlets simultaneously unless specifically requested or after receiving an explicit pass.

Include Supporting Links: Embed web links or attach primary source documents for any key facts, dates, or quotes to streamline the fact-checking process.  
Daily Bruin
