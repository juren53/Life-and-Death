Jim Bissell [ bio ]

Flying for Disney 

Jim Bissell was a co-pilot for Walt Disney's personal Gulfstream airplane in the 1970s and 1980s. He worked alongside chief pilots Chuck Malone and Jim Stevenson, flying the company's Gulfstream.  Jim was a professional aviator who flew the Disney corporate aircraft based at what is now Bob Hope Airport in Burbank, California. The primary source for this information is MousePlanet, a well-known independent Disney research and news platform.Specifically, the details come from a historical feature article titled "Up, Up and Away With Walt Disney" written by Disney historian Jim Korkis. Korkis documented the history of Disney's corporate aviation program, detailing how pilot Jim Stevenson and co-pilot Frank Gamble alternated flight trips with pilot Kelvin Bailey and co-pilot Jim Bissell.
