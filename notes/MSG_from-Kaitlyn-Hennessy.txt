Greetings Kaitlyn - -

It was quite a surprise to receive your recent email but I was pleased
to hear that Jack Hennessy  has grand children.  I'm sure he is
pleased with that too.

The tragic events of that day in September 1966 still bring chills
when I recall them.   They were very close to home in more ways than
one.  We had family friends who worked at and flew out of Burbank
Airport and when I began flying some of my flight training was out of
Burbank.  There wasn't a single time I flew out of Burbank that I
didn't think of your grand father and the brave actions he took on
that day.

I hope the Hennessy's have found some peace in his brave actions. I'm
sure he has.

Sincerely,

Jim U'Ren
